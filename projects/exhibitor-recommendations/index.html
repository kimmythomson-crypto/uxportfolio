02

LLM-Powered Exhibitor Recommendations

Client
RX Global
Year
2023–25
Role
UX Design Lead
Focus
AI/LLM · B2B SaaS

Trade show visitors miss exhibitors they’d want to meet: shows are huge and busy, and existing recommendations relied on patchy exhibitor data. Research at two live events shaped an LLM recommender, built on AWS Bedrock, that visitors rated 8.3/10 for credibility and 8.2/10 for explainability against a target of 6. Its behaviour-based recommendations were adopted into Maps, the navigation tool used by 100+ RX shows. After two years of research and iteration across multiple shows, the work went on to shape a high-value meetings service.

250+

events, and the first AI-native feature across all of them

The idea

Recommendations based on what visitors actually need

RX Global runs 250+ shows a year, and for visitors the number one goal is making connections. But shows are big and busy, few people plan ahead, and recommendations relied on registration data that exhibitors often filled in poorly.

An LLM recommender matches exhibitors by meaning, not keywords, from two sources: what visitors say they need, and how they move around the show.

Research

Rated on the show floor

At two live events, visitors rated each recommendation out of 10 on three pillars. All three cleared the target of 6.

8.3

Credibility

Familiar and trustworthy. 28 ratings, median 9.

7.7

Enlightenment

Helped visitors discover new and unknown exhibitors. 23 ratings, median 9.

8.2

Explainability

The reason for each recommendation was clear. 24 ratings, median 8.5.

Testing

Which summary helps visitors choose?

An A/B/C test on the show floor compared three ways of describing each recommended exhibitor. Short bullet points won.

Roll-out

Finding the right channel

Visitors liked the recommendations, but at the show they didn’t want another app, and before it they were already swamped by show emails.

Behaviour-based recommendations found their home in Maps, the navigation tool used by 100+ RX shows since mid-2024. The model also powers paid VIP matchmaking as part of the ‘At Show Experience’, with roll-out across all shows planned from July 2026.
