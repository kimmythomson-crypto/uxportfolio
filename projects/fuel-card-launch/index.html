01

Fuel Card Launch

Client
UK fuel card provider
Year
2026
Role
UX & Marketing Consultant
Focus
UX audit · UX · Marketing · Copywriting

The brief: grow activated fuel cards from around 1,450 to 5,000. But the application flow was losing 60% of users at screen 3. The studio’s independent UX audit traced it to scroll-heavy screens, a buried value proposition and conflicting savings figures, and the redesigned flow leads with the saving in a few clear steps on one screen. Alongside it, the studio redesigned the B2C website and led copywriting and external communications, from app store listings and business cards to newsletters and a social campaign, tackling the trust issues holding drivers back.

~10 → ~250

applications a week

The new application flow

Four short steps, with the saving shown up front and clear next steps once you’ve applied, from choosing an account type through to a live card tracker.

Screen recording, trimmed and sped up 1.5×. Personal details blurred.
