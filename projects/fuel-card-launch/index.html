01

Fuel Card Launch

Client
UK fuel card provider
Year
2026
Role
UX & Marketing Consultant
Focus
UX audit · UX · Marketing · Copywriting

The brief: grow activated fuel cards from around 1,450 to 5,000. But the application flow was losing 60% of users at screen 3. The studio’s independent UX audit traced it to scroll-heavy screens, a buried value proposition and conflicting savings figures, and the redesigned flow leads with the saving in a few clear steps on one screen. Alongside it, the studio redesigned the B2C website and led copywriting and external communications, from app store listings and business cards to newsletters and a social campaign, tackling the trust issues holding drivers back.

~10 → ~250

applications a week

The audit

Where the funnel lost people

An expert review of the live app mapped the fuel card journey screen by screen. The value was there, but it was hidden behind small print, long scrolling copy and two different savings figures.

Recommendations

Six quick wins: high impact, low effort

01

Fix screen 3

Replace the bullet list with icons and short headlines, remove the scroll, and lead with the £ saving.

02

Align the savings figure

19p or 7p: pick one and use it everywhere. Inconsistency is a trust killer.

03

Merge the two sign-up flows

One clear journey, application or waitlist, not both running in parallel.

04

Fix the button hierarchy

On the upsell modal, ‘See how it works’ becomes the primary button and ‘Maybe later’ a text link.

05

Show the card price at the pump

Display the card price next to the pump price at the moment of decision. It sells the card without a word of copy.

06

Label the fuel type filter

Show ‘Diesel ▾’ persistently, tap to change, and remove any doubt about what drivers are seeing.

Longer term

A profile that fills in the application

Drivers already share their vehicle, email and company details across the app. Collecting them gradually, and showing what each step unlocks, turns the credit application into a final check rather than a form.

  1. 01

    Add vehicle to Garage

    Unlocks fuel card compatibility on the map.

  2. 02

    Verify email

    Unlocks exclusive offers.

  3. 03

    Add company name and address

    Unlocks fleet invoicing.

  4. 04

    Apply now

    The form is already filled in. Just a credit check.

The new application flow

Four short steps, with the saving shown up front and clear next steps once you’ve applied, from choosing an account type through to a live card tracker.

Screen recording, trimmed and sped up 1.5×. Personal details blurred.
