03

mirrOR Surgical Interface Redesign

Client
Rods & Cones
Year
2021
Role
UX Design Lead
Focus
MedTech

mirrOR lets surgeons stream their point of view to remote experts mid-operation, but years of added features had left a toolbar packed with controls competing for attention at exactly the wrong moment. Six interviews across surgeons, support agents and product managers, plus an icon recognition test, showed users relied on just five or six of them. The redesign cut the bottom toolbar to six core controls and moved everything else into collapsible panels, without removing any functionality.

100%

of Harvard Medical School surgeons chose the new interface
