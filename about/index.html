About

A studio focusing on UX, product design and marketing. Founded in 2014, based in London.

The studio

Kim Sum Studio (金心工作室) began in 2014 as a branding and illustration practice. In 2018 it moved into UX, and today it works across UX, product design and marketing, from research and audits through to shipped interfaces and the campaigns that bring people to them.

We work with in-house teams on focused engagements: finding where a product loses people, designing what fixes it, and making sure the story outside the product matches the experience inside it.

Founder

Kimmy Thomson is a UX and product designer with a background spanning branding, graphic design, illustration, architecture and emerging technology. She was previously Design Lead in RX Global’s innovation team, working on LLM-powered recommendations, AR experiences and exhibitor analytics. Through the studio, she now works with in-house teams and startups to sharpen their UX and marketing.

She has lived and worked in London, Amsterdam, Madrid and Hong Kong, and writes the Substack Kim Sum Diaries.
